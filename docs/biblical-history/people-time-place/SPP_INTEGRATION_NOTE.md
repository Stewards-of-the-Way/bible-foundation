# SPP Integration: one shared research layer

Purpose: provide one reusable people/event/time/place evidence source for SPP. The sermon-specific Info Map remains the one canonical sermon record.

1. Identify referenced persons and relationships from the sermon’s significant Scripture in context.
2. Lookup the shared person ID, geography, chronology, and evidence; do not duplicate an established person or replace Pastor Jake’s sermon evidence with library claims.
3. Preserve Scripture textual-witness lines: Matthew 1 and Luke 3 in distinct views; Masoretic Genesis ages with one selected, named BC/AD overlay.
4. **Do not add people/relations to the pinned permanent master during ordinary sermon processing.** Research any material missing relationships in the canonical sermon evidence layers; distinguish direct stated family bonds from genealogy succession and competing scholarly hypotheses. A true master correction requires separate owner review and versioned release.
5. When biblical lifespans overlap, calculate potential contemporaneity; NEVER assert acquaintanceship absent text.
6. Pair time and place when available. If either is missing or contested, record precision and proceed. Do not invent geocodes.
7. A sermon Info Map may link shared person IDs and an optional map/time/tree view. This library must not become a second sermon record. SPP completeness is never blocked by irrelevant missing family trees.
8. BC and AD are used for display; do not treat Ussher BC overlay as scripture-established absolute chronology.
