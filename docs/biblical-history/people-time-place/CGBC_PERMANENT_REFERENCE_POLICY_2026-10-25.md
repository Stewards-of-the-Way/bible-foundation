## Critical distinction: pinned but non-exhaustive

**The approved library is an authoritative CGBC reference snapshot for records it contains, but it is NOT the entire Scripture/history/geography research universe.** It is particularly useful for genealogies, ages, overlap calculations, known biblical identities and already documented chronology/geography. It may omit historical setting, chronology, cultures, polities, legal and economic facts, archaeological context, place evidence, rulers, routes and events associated with Scripture passages **even when there is no genealogy or known person**. A missing record is never, by itself, a reason to stop research or mark time/place/history `NOT_APPLICABLE`.

Read `AMENDMENT_2026-10-25_REFERENCE_FIRST_NOT_SOURCE_LIMIT.md` for the required reference-first **then independently research gaps** method and completion gate. **Stable reference does not mean frozen scholarship or limited study.** Continue the full v1.8 and REV5 approved source stack, including BibleHub research/link-out and fit-for-purpose geography, chronology and archaeology references; never imply their material is reproduced without rights.

## What SPP still must do in each sermon

1. **Independently study meaningful Scripture:** immediate passage context, original Hebrew/Aramaic/Greek as appropriate, canonical/scriptural relationships, textual chronology and original-language evidence **before** following outside commentary. Evaluate material context, event dates, book-composition dates and real locations in proportion to the sermon.
2. **Reuse the reference as supporting context:** resolve existing person, family, event, timeline, Scripture, place and map references by stable ID. Retain separate BC/AD and Genesis-relative chronology, date-model alternatives, spatial precision, archaeological qualifications and source/rights/provenance. Overlapping lifetimes do **not** demonstrate personal contact. Matthew 1 and Luke 3 remain distinct scriptural lists. Never invent missing coordinates, a parable location or an exact historical date.
3. **Independently research beyond the reference whenever relevant:** BibleHub and the approved primary Scripture, lexical, commentary, cultural, social, legal/economic, historical, archaeological, ancient-geographic, chronology and mapping source stack are part of normal SPP, **especially when the library lacks a subject, person, date, location, event or passage-specific context**. Do not stop after a library hit if important information is still missing. The absence of genealogy never exempts a passage from researching its historical time/place setting. When outside evidence disagrees with the reference, record and source the disagreement in that sermon's research/evidence; do not silently rewrite the permanent library.
4. **Publish one canonical sermon Info Map** and appropriate local research, links, Discover/Deep Dive, contextual words, quote, Author Corpus and source/evidence layers. Keep exact Jake evidence, faithful Jake synthesis, independent study, and external research separate. Routine research discoveries belong in the sermon and its legitimate source/evidence records, not an unreviewed change to the reference baseline.
5. **No forced exhaustive geography or genealogy:** a meaningful unavailable-date, alternate model, unresolved place, unsupported branch or `NOT_APPLICABLE` decision is valid when explicitly qualified. Research completeness is evidence- and relevance-based, not a false-precision count.

This is a **separate reviewable library revision**, not an SPP weekly task. Preserve prior IDs and citations; record what changed, reason, evidence and source/license checks, chronology hypotheses, migration effects, reviewer/authorization, new version and checksums. Rebuild the three coordinated projections **together**; regression-test all IDs, references, licenses, old Info Maps and geography precision. Do not overwrite the pinned baseline just because a web page changed.

## Scope and rights

This policy freezes an **approved research snapshot**, not the biblical/historical truth or third-party licenses. Maintain CC BY 4.0 attribution, MIT/ISC notices and excluded-content restrictions as documented in the foundation package; these do not become public domain by being locally pinned. A candidate location's plotted point is not proof of where an event occurred. External source text/imagery requires independent item-level publication clearance.
