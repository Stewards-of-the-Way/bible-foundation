## Known biblical interpretation issues, kept visible

1. **Esther** was central to deliverance of the Jews in Persia (Esther 4–9). **Ruth** is in the David/Jesus ancestry (Ruth 4, Matthew 1). Esther is not inserted into Jesus’ genealogy.
2. **Matthew 1 and Luke 3 must remain separate**. Luke does not expressly identify his line as Mary’s. Joseph has differing listed ancestors. Source-scoped IDs preserve unresolved identity questions.
3. Luke’s **Cainan** between Arphaxad and Shelah differs from Genesis 11’s Masoretic numeric genealogy. Do not mix that branch into the Genesis relative-year arithmetic without a separate text-model calculation.
4. **Adam and Methuselah overlap by 243 modeled years** in the retained Genesis data. This does not prove they met. Noah was born later than Adam’s modeled death.
5. Ussher **4004 BC** is **one chronology overlay**, not an independently verified Creation date. Label all such dates by model and keep relative-year calculation unchanged.
6. Unknown parentage, dating, or geometry is an ordinary valid research state, **not a weekly SPP blocker**.
